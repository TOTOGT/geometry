Theorem W.1
The Wigner crystallization is G = U∘F∘K∘C acting on the 2D electron gas manifold X. At rs = rs* ≈ 30–40, the Jacobian loses rank by 1 (translational mode softens). The triangular lattice is the stable fixed point of the dm³ flow.
Status · 2026-10-01
Checked and correct: rs* ≈ 30–40 brackets both standing quantum Monte Carlo estimates — Tanatar & Ceperley (PRB 39:5005, 1989) give rs ≈ 37 ± 5, Drummond & Needs (PRL 102:126402, 2009) give rs = 31(1). The triangular ground state is Bonsall & Maradudin (PRB 15:1959, 1977). The B-field control raises effective rs, which is the right direction: Landau quantisation suppresses kinetic energy and so favours the crystal. Its label was reworded, since "Landau suppression" reads as suppression of the crystal.

Modelling choice, not derived fact: the order of the 2D fluid-to-crystal transition is open — Drummond & Needs note it has been argued the transition cannot be first order, and do not settle it. A fold is a continuous, soft-mode picture, so Theorem W.1's "translational mode softens" picks one side of an unsettled question. Separately, breaking continuous translation in 2D gives two acoustic phonon branches, so "loses rank by 1" is the house corank-1 F-operator convention rather than the count the physics gives. Both open; neither assumed elsewhere.

μmax = −0.31, ω = 0.19 and β = 1.7 above are asserted dm³ parameters with no derivation on this page or in Lean. Nothing else uses them.
