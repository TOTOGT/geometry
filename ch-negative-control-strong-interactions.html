A negative control: the April 2026 strong-interaction paper

This four-page paper is kept as a negative control: a paper whose claims outrun its proofs, used to test whether the series' checks can say so. It claimed that nuclear matter is the dm³ cycle iterated along a Reeb flow, with a Tribonacci fractal measure and a g = 33 lock-in. This chapter reads it one claim at a time.

The paper

Source. TOTOGT/AXLE, AnuclearPhysics/NuclearPhysicsB_latex.pdf (4 pages, 271,149 bytes, md5 7961116c2967103990c5a4fac3fc5f45), the file this chapter reads; LaTeX source beside it as TOGTnuclearPhysicsB.latex. The paper's Lean companion is grossi-ops/cajueiro/lean/NuclearPhysicsB/ (nine files; TribonacciMeasure.lean is byte-identical to AXLE/TribonacciMeasure.lean). By R22, Lean outside geometry is a claim nobody here can check until it moves in.

“Topographical Orthogenetic Generative Theory in Strong Interactions: Contact Geometry, dm³ Operator Cycle, and Fold Events in Nuclear Matter”, P. N. Grossi, G6 LLC, 4 pages, dated 11 April 2026. It is a different paper from the Version 4 TOGT paper (“seven proofs of the Tribonacci constant”); this one applies the framework to quarks, gluons and quark-gluon plasma.

Its argument: model the nuclear medium as a contact 3-manifold with a Reeb field Rα; let the cycle G = U ∘ F ∘ K ∘ C act along it; get the recurrence w(k+3) = w(k+2) + w(k+1) + w(k) with dominant root η ≈ 1.839; weight by η−k; read hadronization as fold events; state four falsifiability conditions.

What the control shows

A negative control is only worth keeping if the checks can fail on it. Of the ten rows above, three hold: the Tribonacci root and the contracting weights (kernel-audited) and Figure 1's scale (read from the drawing). Seven are flagged: one identification stated and not derived, one wrong name, one overstated verification claim, one “measure” that is a sequence, one threshold whose own Lean statement ends in sorry, one interpretation with no computation, and one set of predictions that cannot count until it is pre-registered. So the checks did fail on it, in each of the ways the series says it looks for: a true theorem attached to a claim it does not support, a number carrying the wrong name, and a prediction with no test of its own power.

How the absences were looked for (R15). “Not derived” and “not a measure” rest on reading all nine files of the Lean companion, searching geometry and AXLE by content for the recurrence and for Tribonacci in any case, and listing every file named for it on the Desktop. They are statements about what I found, not proof that no derivation exists elsewhere.

What the control does not show is that the framework is false. The held rows are true and the flagged rows are untested or unsupported, which is a different thing. A positive control, a paper whose claims all hold, would be the other half of the pair, and none is claimed here.

Foundations

R21 says an evidence tag on a chapter that stands on an untagged assumption is decoration, so the base comes first. Everything proved above is a fact about the cubic x³ = x² + x + 1 and the sequence it governs. None of it is a fact about quarks, gluons or a contact manifold. What the paper needs underneath:

  1. The identification. That G generates the Tribonacci recurrence is stated, in the paper and in docstrings, and I did not find it derived. Without it the proved rows are true and unconnected to the paper's subject.
  2. The space. The Lean companion defines an abstract GenerativeManifold (a metric carrier, a functional Φ, a field) and a ContactPair, but no nuclear medium is an instance of either. The paper's “effective contact form on the QCD vacuum” is therefore a name for a hypothesis, not an object.
  3. The operators. C, K, F and U are defined in Operators.lean as structures that bundle their own properties as fields (C contractive and injective, K non-increasing in Φ, F with a finite branch locus, U decreasing Φ with a stable branch). That makes the composite G a well-formed statement and nothing more: the properties are assumed of any instance, and no instance is given. The paper itself describes the four in words only.

Until these are discharged, the tags in the table above apply to the arithmetic only.

Figure 1

The figure shows five rings stacked along the Reeb axis, each wider than the last by the factor η. Reading the vector drawing directly, the factor is 1.8393 on every step, so the figure does what its caption says. Two cautions. It is a schematic, so the rings carry no data. And the rings are levels along the Reeb direction, not orbits: a Reeb flow holds its angle fixed and preserves contact volume, so the picture must not be read as an advancing helix. That is the same reason the series withdrew its “helical phase” prediction.

What the book lets us do now

The April paper left several parts it could not defend. Looking through the series for each one:

Net: of the parts April could not defend, one can be settled in the negative today (the lock-in), one can be replaced by a precise and less flattering statement (the measure), and the rest are unchanged. None of it moves a row up. It moves two rows from “stated” to “disproved as stated” and “constructed, not fractal”, which is what a negative control is for.

What this chapter does not do

It does not prove or disprove that nuclear matter follows the cycle. The proved part is the arithmetic of the Tribonacci constant. Everything that would make it physics, a measure on a real space, a proof of the 33-generation lock-in, a connection to a QCD observable, is still open (in the files I read), and the paper's own falsifiability conditions cannot be tested until they are pre-registered.
