On 2026-09-19 a scan of the author's Downloads folder found Weibel's K-book, van Neerven's Functional Analysis, Connes and Marcolli, and Deisenroth's Mathematics for Machine Learning — 5,309 pages of primary source, none of it cited anywhere in this corpus. The floor WP-82 asked for was not missing. It had no address.

1 · Why XVII and not XI

WP-82 §3 assigns XI–XVI to rungs 28–33 of the “33 Levels” graphic: K-theory, operator algebras, higher category theory, derived algebraic geometry, motivic homotopy, noncommutative geometry. This volume takes none of them, and the reason is worth stating rather than assumed.

The mathematics of machine learning is not a rung on that ladder. It is not a step above noncommutative geometry; it sits underneath the entire programme, which is exactly why it measured at zero. A scan on 2026-09-17 found pytorch in one tracked file, jax in two, and reinforcement learning, program synthesis and tensorflow in none — against numpy in 89 and scipy in 56. The corpus computes; it does not learn.

The same argument WP-82 made, one floor lower WP-82's finding was that the corpus reached rung 33 in thirty-one file-mentions and rung 28 in zero — spectral triples resting on a K-theory that was never written. An operator programme aimed at machine-assisted mathematics, with no machine learning in it, has the identical shape. The measurement is the same; only the floor is lower.

2 · The bar this volume has to clear

WP-82 set an admissibility rule for Part II: each volume needs a machine-checked core, “or it is a reading list with a DOI on it.” That rule applies here and is met differently, because this volume claims no theorems. Its obligation is address discipline:

  • Every page number is located by a script against the file named in docs/floor-texts.tsv, matched on the printed page. None is copied from memory or from another document.
  • Every chapter carries a verify script that re-runs the location and exits non-zero if a row moves. A different printing moves the numbers, and the script says so rather than failing silently.
  • Each text is identified by (path, sha256, page count), not by title. A title is a name; an address is a name plus a place plus a hash.

No text is reproduced here. The books are the author's own copies and stay that way; what this volume publishes is the map between them and the corpus.

5 · What this volume does not claim

  • No new mathematics. Every result quoted belongs to the text it is cited from.
  • No claim that the corpus's objects are the standard ones. Chapter 1 asserts a correspondence of vocabulary and says which side each term came from.
  • No volume numeral is taken from WP-82's plan. XI, XII, XIII and XVI are spoken for, and book13/ already collides with XIII; XVII was free.
  • Nothing here is machine-checked, because nothing here is a theorem. The verify scripts check addresses, which is the only thing this volume asserts.
