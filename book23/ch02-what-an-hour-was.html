What an Hour Was

O que foi uma hora — the time between a warning and what followed, counted in hours.
For the patients and their families, each hour without the care they needed mattered. This chapter counts only what the dates allow. It does not count deaths, and it does not estimate harm per hour.

Row 6 uses 8 January, the date the Attorney General’s Office gave the Supreme Court; with 10 or 17 January the interval would be shorter.

Small hours

A university hospital in Manaus went about four hours without oxygen on 14 January, according to an anonymous doctor speaking to Estadão (Estado de Minas, 14 Jan 2021). A family told g1 they were asked to bring an oxygen tank to the hospital and paid 500 reais to refill one after four days caring for their mother at home (Notícias ao Minuto, 15 Jan 2021). Both are accounts from individuals, reported by the press, and not independently checked.
