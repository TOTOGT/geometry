Preface

Book XXVII is a study book for the University of the People MSIT program, written as chapters in the series style: each chapter maps an official course, explains the standard material in its own words, and says plainly what it did and did not read. The business core comes first because it is the part of the program that is mostly reading and argument; the computing core follows.

The book is a study aid and not a way to complete graded work. It contains no answers to assignments, it cites no course material it has not read, and it asks you to write your own submissions in your own words.
