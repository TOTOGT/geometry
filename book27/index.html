The MSIT Study Book

A chapter for each course of the University of the People MSIT program, with the business core first. Each chapter maps the official units, explains the standard material, and marks what it has and has not read.

Preface

Book XXVII is a study book for the University of the People MSIT program, written as chapters in the series style: each chapter maps an official course, explains the standard material in its own words, and says plainly what it did and did not read. The business core comes first because it is the part of the program that is mostly reading and argument; the computing core follows.

The book is a study aid and not a way to complete graded work. It contains no answers to assignments, it cites no course material it has not read, and it asks you to write your own submissions in your own words.

Capstone rule, as pasted to confirm

The catalog text says a grade below B- allows one retake, and failing the second attempt ends eligibility for degree conferral. The Capstone is taken last, after all core courses and at least three electives. Confirm the current wording in the official catalog before planning around it.

The sample learning pathway

The catalog's example sequence for a student with no transfer credit is, in order: BUS 5113, MSIT 5210, 5212, 5214, 5216, BUS 5115, MSIT 5226, 5250, BUS 5611, MSIT 5260, 5270, MSIT 5910. Your own pathway may differ. This book keeps the business core first because you asked for it, not because the pathway does.

Open to confirm
