Drinfeld & Beilinson

Drinfeld proved the Langlands correspondence for GL(2) over function fields by building the geometry it lives on. Beilinson and Drinfeld then asked what that geometry says when the arithmetic is removed entirely.

Vladimir Drinfeld, b. 1954 · Fields Medal 1990 · Alexander Beilinson · Wolf Prize 2018, shared

Part I · Why geometry

A curve over a finite field is a number field that holds still

The previous chapter named three strands. The function-field one is the one where proofs came first, and the reason is the second column of Weil's Rosetta stone: a curve C over a finite field Fq has a geometry, and a number field does not. The objects the correspondence talks about — automorphic forms on GL(n) — become functions on the set of rank-n vector bundles on C, and bundles can be moduli-counted, compared, and degenerated.

Drinfeld's route to GL(2) ran through objects he invented for the purpose: elliptic modules (now Drinfeld modules), and then shtukas, which are bundles with a Frobenius-twisted gluing. The Galois side of the correspondence is carved out of their cohomology. That is the content of the function-field proof, and nothing in this corpus reproduces it.

Part II · The step to sheaves

BunG, and what Beilinson and Drinfeld asked of it

Replace “functions on bundles over Fq” by “sheaves on the moduli stack BunG of G-bundles on a curve”, and the finite field drops out of the statement. A function becomes a sheaf by Grothendieck's dictionary — the function is the trace of Frobenius on the stalks — and the arithmetic content is recovered by specialising back. What remains is a conjecture about complex curves. On one side, D-modules (or sheaves) on BunG; on the other, local systems for the dual group LG. The correspondence sends a local system to a Hecke eigensheaf, a sheaf that the Hecke operators merely rescale by the local system's own monodromy.

Beilinson and Drinfeld built such eigensheaves for a special class of local systems, the opers, by quantising the Hitchin integrable system on the cotangent bundle of BunG. The same opers are what Feigin and Frenkel find at the critical level, which is why ch-feigin and this chapter describe two ends of one construction: the centre of the affine algebra at critical level on a disc, and the differential operators on BunG on a whole curve.

The repair. The conjecture as first formulated — an eigensheaf for every irreducible local system — turned out to be too weak to be true as a categorical statement. Arinkin and Gaitsgory restored it in 2012 by adding a condition on the singular support of the sheaves allowed. That is the version Gaitsgory, Raskin and their coauthors proved in 2024. See the previous chapter for the account.

Part III · The part you can check

The volume of Bun2 on the projective line

Nothing about the correspondence itself is checkable by hand. One number about the space it lives on is. Over Fq, every rank-2 bundle on P1 is a sum of two line bundles O(a) ⊕ O(b) (Grothendieck, 1957), so the groupoid is completely enumerable. Count each bundle E with weight 1/|Aut E|, which is how a stack is counted. For a > b the automorphism group has order (q−1)² · qa−b+1 (two units on the diagonal, and Hom(O(b), O(a)) = H0(O(a−b)) of dimension a−b+1); for a = b it is GL2(Fq).

The Siegel–Harder mass formula says the whole sum, in any fixed degree, is q−3/(q−1) · ζP1(2), where ζP1(s) = 1/((1−q−s)(1−q1−s)). The script enumerates and compares in exact rational arithmetic:

degree 0:   1/|GL_2(F_q)| + sum_{a>=1} 1/((q-1)^2 q^(2a+1))
degree 1:   sum_{a>=1} 1/((q-1)^2 q^(2a))
both equal  q^-3 / (q-1) * zeta_P1(2)         for every q tested

They match for eighteen field sizes from 2 to 32, in every degree from −3 to 5, with the infinite series compared against both its closed form and an 80-bundle partial sum. Multiplying the mass by q4 gives 1.002 at q = 103 and 1.000000002 at 109: the stack has dimension (g−1)·dim GL2 = −4, which is the one place the phrase “dimension of the moduli stack” is a number you can watch converge. Four controls run through the same code — a wrong automorphism order, a dropped ζ factor, a wrong Hom dimension, and the rank-3 mass — and each must fail to match. They do.

What this does not show. It is a statement about the size of Bun2, not about sheaves on it. On P1 there are no unramified cusp forms and no irreducible unramified local systems, so the correspondence is vacuous in genus 0; the computation is the geometry before the theorem starts. Higher genus is where BunG has the Hitchin system and the opers, and nothing here reaches it. The check does not engage Beilinson–Drinfeld's construction or Arinkin–Gaitsgory's repair.

Part IV · What this corpus does not have

Rung thirty-two, second occupant

WP-130 found Drinfeld and Beilinson at zero occurrences, and the single mention of Gaitsgory in a list of sources to obtain. This chapter gives them names and one number; it does not build the correspondence. Taken with Langlands and Feigin, the gallery now attributes the arithmetic strand, the geometric strand's formulation, and the vertex-algebra route through the critical level. It still occupies none of them.
