How to read this: The Ulam spiral places integers in a square outward coil, centre = 1. Primes are denser along the diagonals (on the 101 × 101 grid, 25.1% of the cells on the two main diagonals are prime, against 12.0% elsewhere). Euler's n² + n + 41 lies on the two main diagonals only when the spiral is started at 41, not at 1 as here. Spiral B is the same spiral as A, rotated as a picture, not a second lattice. At 0° it lies exactly on A: every prime is a coincidence (1,252 of 1,252 on the 101 × 101 grid). Cell positions coincide exactly only at rotations of 90°: 359 of 1,252 primes coincide at 90° and 270°, 292 at 180°, against about 154 if the two were independent. At every other angle on the slider (steps of 0.5°) the rotated cells only partly overlap the grid cells; only −180°, −90°, 0°, 90° and 180° are exact.

Connection to the series: The dm³ fold radius r* ≈ 0.776 is not a 7th-order Chebyshev node: 2cos(3π/7) = 0.445, and the nearest node of T₇ is 0.7818. Whether the prime diagonals are a discrete analogue of a Whitney A₁ caustic, and the link to galactic lensing (→ WP58, WP59), are MODEL statements, not shown here.
