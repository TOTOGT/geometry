Every scientist in this volume is an operator. The chain G = U∘F∘K∘C was written by nature before it was written in mathematics — these chapters show who was watching when it happened.

C · seed  ∘  K · curvature  ∘  F · fold  ∘  U · expansion  =  τ = 2

G7 is the seventh application of the operator G to itself — the volume where the mathematics meets the mathematicians. Each chapter reads one scientist's life and work through the dm³ lens: which operator were they? Where did the chain close for them? What sorry did they live with, and did they name it?
