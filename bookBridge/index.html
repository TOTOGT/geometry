The Bridge

A translation tool for transitions: how a scientist who understands one field can ask whether the same questions, in the same order, can be put to another. Built from the Coherence Bridge of Books III to VI, with the failures kept in, because they are what make the rest believable.

Preface

The idea behind the series is to think about systems and about putting them back together. The Bridge is that idea made into a tool. Each chapter takes one more field through the same list of questions, which is why the structure repeats and why readers are asked to try it themselves on a field they know. Some fields skip a question, and the book says which.

The Bridge carries a shape, the fold and its exponents, and a list of questions. It does not carry constants or mechanism, and nothing here claims that different fields share an underlying cause. Where a chapter claims more than the evidence supports, the page says so on the page.

Language and grammar attempts, the models that try to do the mathematics needed to reintegrate the sciences, belong here too, as chapters that state what the shared language can and cannot do. The manifesto is a separate book.
