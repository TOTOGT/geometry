PREDICTIONS -- stated up front
------------------------------
The paper's falsifiable content was scattered across 5, 7.2 and 8 and easy to
miss. It now leads: a block before 1 stating six commitments, each signed, each
with the observation that would refute it.

  P1  lambda_bar = ((1-delta)/delta)*gamma/(1-gamma) = 0.0186 per period. A
      point value with nothing fitted to produce it -- beta, tau, c_K and kappa
      all cancel.
  P2  d2 W_J/d sigma d lambda < 0. The kink, timed to the collapse of lambda
      rather than to the price move.
  P3  Four signs from Corollary 1, from the implicit function theorem at the
      crossing, not from estimation.
  P4  d lambda_bar/d delta < 0. Patience cuts the other way.
  P5  lambda-jumps must reverse the sign that lambda-collapses produce. Five
      events sharing one sign fit many theories; the same parameter reversing
      the sign fits one.
  P6  gamma > 0 or no threshold exists at any volatility. Position must inform,
      not merely cost.

The block closes with what is deliberately not claimed: sigma* is not predicted
to be 1/3 or to be any constant, and even lambda_bar is asserted only as a
formula and the sign of its derivative, not as a distinguished value. Stating
the non-claims next to the claims is the point -- it is what 7.2 retired.
