Two Conversations

What a year of applications has produced so far.
This chapter says what the record shows and what it does not. Where something is not known, the chapter says so and does not fill the gap.

What the record holds

The index lists applications, or sets of application materials, prepared from August 2026. It is built from saved files, so it is not a count of anything: the files do not show which applications were sent, applications made with one click on a job board leave no file, and applications made since 21 October are only partly in it. The applicant is not counting, and this page claims no number of applications, sent or prepared. The true number is higher than the rows shown. No outcome is recorded for any of them.

The two conversations

Over the same period, two people spoke with the applicant about roles. One of those conversations was with a person at a nonprofit in disability services, and the applicant is writing that person a thank-you note. The other is not described here beyond the fact that it happened. One of the two people said they would let the applicant know within a few days, and at the time of writing there is no answer, so that outcome is open and is recorded as open.

The chapter does not say which of the two is the one still waiting for an answer. It does not name either person or either employer.

What the numbers allow

Two conversations out of an unknown number of submissions does not yield a rate. Reporting one would require the missing count, and any figure given without it would be invented. The saved files do allow a smaller statement: the applications they record span roughly a dozen or more industries by the inferred labels in the index, and the search has produced two conversations that the applicant counts, one of them still open. The files only start in August, so the search itself may be older than the record.

The counts do not support a statement about the applicant. The same pattern would describe a strong candidate in a slow market or a weak fit between a résumé and a posting, and the data here cannot tell those apart. The record only fixes what happened so that later chapters can add to it.

Updates since 21 October

These entries were added on 9 November 2026. They are stated as the applicant reported them. Nothing below changes the list in the index, which was built from August to October files and has since had only the rows marked in its Sent? column added.

  • In October, a graduate teaching program approved the applicant. The applicant is not yet working in a school, and has not said whether the approval has been accepted.
  • The next step in that program is an interview with a partner school. Three schools invited the applicant to record video interviews. At the last report one was recorded and two were still to do; the status of the other two since then is not recorded here.
  • One interview with a school has since taken place. In it the applicant was asked which of the school’s stated values he identified with, and answered that he identified with two and disagreed in part with the third. The chapter does not name the school or the values. No answer from the school has come, so the outcome is open and is recorded as open.

Whether a recorded or live school interview counts as a conversation in the sense of the section above has not been decided. Until it is, the chapter still reports two conversations, and these interviews are listed apart from them. The title and the count are left unchanged until the applicant decides.

What would change this chapter

  • Confirming which listed applications were sent, and when, fills in the Sent? column. It does not turn the list into a count.
  • The answer to the open conversation closes it, in either direction.
  • Applications made through job boards with one click, once listed, add rows.
