Book XXVI · Chapter 9 · The cost of the gate

A Licence Before a Student

What New Jersey asks of a one-person company that would sell instruction, and what the asking costs before anyone has enrolled.
What this is

A record of one written reply from a State agency, received 2026-10-26, and of what its requirements cost. It is not legal advice, and it is not a determination: the reply says in terms that the agency cannot tell from what it was sent whether this company is covered. No application has been filed. No instruction is being sold. The officer who wrote is left unnamed, as employers are elsewhere in this book; the unit is named because the unit is the authority.

What the gate is

One statute and two administrative codes. N.J.S.A. 34:15C-10.1 defines a private career school and assigns approval jointly to the Department of Labor and Workforce Development and the Department of Education. N.J.A.C. 12:41 is Labor’s code: initial application, renewal, performance reporting, financial soundness. N.J.A.C. 6A:19 is Education’s: instructional, operational and programmatic standards.

The reply came from the Training Evaluation Unit, inside the Center for Occupational Employment Information, within the Office of Research and Information. Three names for three nested rooms, which is itself a fact about the size of the thing one is approaching.

The test, and the three ways out

The definition is four-part and every part must hold at once. A qualifying school (a) offers instruction preparing people for future education or the workplace, (b) by any method, classroom or correspondence or internet, (c) to the general public or through the State workforce system, and (d) charges tuition, fees or costs, or receives public funding for it. Fail any one of the four and the definition does not reach you.

Three exclusions follow. Colleges and institutions already licensed under some other law of the State are out. An employer instructing its own employees is out, provided the employee pays nothing and the employer profits nothing. And instruction that is avocational, cultural or recreational in nature is out.

Where the line falls

The same lecture is inside or outside the definition depending on what it is sold as. Mathematics sold as enrichment is cultural. The same mathematics sold as preparation for a job is occupational, and occupational instruction with a price on it is a school. The statute does not ask what was taught. It asks what the teaching was for, and whether money changed hands.

The requirements are listed at N.J.A.C. 12:41-2.2, beginning at page 7 of Labor’s code. Filing is through the State’s grants platform. Every figure above falls due before a first student has enrolled, and none of it is returned if none ever does. That is the shape of the thing: the cost is fixed, paid forward, and indifferent to demand.

What approval buys, and what it does not

The private career school application contains the application for the Eligible Training Provider List; there is no second form and no second fee. On approval a Certificate is issued jointly by both departments, the programs are added to the List, and they become visible on the State’s training site, which the agency describes as visited by thousands of residents each week. Listed programs may also qualify for federal Individual Training Account funding and for State grants.

The agency’s traffic figure is its own and is recorded here unverified. Even taken at face value it is a count of visitors, not of enrolments. Approval converts a prohibition into a permission and a company into a listing. It does not deliver a student, and nothing in the statute or either code pretends otherwise.

There is one cheaper door, and it is only open to those already through another: an organisation already licensed as a school by one of several named agencies may file the Eligible Training Provider application alone, at no cost. The list of those agencies did not come through in the message received.

What was asked, and what came back

The question put was narrow: what status would be required, and what would listing involve. The reply was a map of the law rather than a ruling on the asker, and it said so: it is difficult to say with certainty, on the information provided, whether the organisation meets the definition. That sentence is the most useful one in the message. It marks where a free written determination could be obtained, on a precise description of a specific offering, before $1,150 becomes non-refundable.

No such description has been sent. Nothing is being taught at present, so there is nothing yet to describe.

Not established here
  • That this company is, or is not, a private career school. No determination was requested and none was issued.
  • That the fee and bond figures hold beyond the date of the message they were read in. They are quoted as received, not checked against the codes.
  • That the training site’s weekly traffic is what the agency states. It is recorded as the agency’s claim.
  • Anything about whether approval would be worth its price. That depends on students, and there are none.
  • The identity of the agencies whose licence permits the cheaper route. That list was missing from the message.
