Working across government banking, institutional finance, entrepreneurship, education and commercial operations has given me a perspective on how organizations function beyond their individual departments.

My current research builds on that experience by examining the relationships between financial decisions, institutional structures, operational processes and human outcomes.

I am particularly interested in developing analytical methods that help organizations identify dependencies, anticipate unintended consequences and improve coordination across specialized functions.

Lead evidenceThe Economics of Pretrial Detention: A Descriptive Audit

Research finding · Python · saved outputs and source-file hashes
+7.6 pointshigher early-plea rate within 30 days when bail was not posted, among matched New York local-court misdemeanors with bail above $1. Descriptive, not causal.

Compares cases with the same charge, court, year, criminal-history category and bail band. Sensitivity checks, including restricting the sample to earlier cohorts, are reported in the chapter.

1,028,073 court records → 685,457 local-court misdemeanors → 17,289 with bail above $1 through September 2025 → 5,755 matched cases (4,778 bail not posted, 977 posted).

The full sample flow, alternative specifications, limitations and a judge-level check that does not support a stronger design are set out in the chapter.
