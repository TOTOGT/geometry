1. Germany

QuestionWhat the sources establishStatus and source type
Foreign degree and licenceThe state health authority (Approbationsbehörde) of the state where you intend to work decides. It first checks whether your training is equivalent (Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung). If it finds essential differences that experience does not offset, you take a Kenntnisprüfung: an oral-practical exam of 60 to 90 minutes with patient cases (internal medicine, surgery, emergency medicine, imaging, clinical pharmacology, radiation protection, legal questions). It may be repeated at most twice. [1][2]VERIFIED Official chamber guidance, not the statute itself. [1] is a 2023 snapshot, and [2] is dated February 2017 and says it has no binding legal force.
Foreign specialty trainingThe state medical chamber (Landesärztekammer) assesses content and duration of your Weiterbildung individually. Partial crediting of training periods is possible. If essential differences remain after considering home-country practice, a Facharztprüfung may be required. Non-EU specialist diplomas are not automatically recognised. The chamber needs you to be a member, which needs an Approbation or Berufserlaubnis. [1][2]PARTIAL How much credit an ophthalmologist from Brazil would typically get is OPEN. Neither page mentions Brazil or ophthalmology.
Binding vs model rulesThe federal chamber's model specialty regulation is advisory ("only recommendatory character"). Only the regulation of your own state chamber binds you. [3]VERIFIED Federal chamber statement. One state's ophthalmology extract lists 60 months of training [4]; other states were not checked.
Independent practiceMedical work needs a valid Approbation or Berufserlaubnis. The Approbation is the licence to practise independently, valid nationwide without time limit. [1][2]VERIFIED
LASIK-specific competenceThe KRC (a joint commission of the German ophthalmological society and the ophthalmologists' professional association) recommends a recognised theory course, observation with a recognised trainer, and first operations with that trainer present. Listing needs recognition as an ophthalmology specialist. The text calls itself recommendations; no statement that it is law was found. [5] A state chamber extract lists indication and diagnostics for refractive procedures, not LASIK operations as surgeon. [4]VERIFIED professional guidance. Whether a clinic or insurer requires KRC listing: OPEN.
Language, immigrationGeneral German at B2, and a medical language exam at about C1 run by the state chambers (doctor-patient talk, doctor-doctor handover, written medical report). [1][2] Immigration and work-permit rules were not read: OPEN.PARTIAL
While being evaluatedThe Berufserlaubnis is a temporary permit for people who finished medical training. The 2017 guide says it can be granted or extended for at most two years, can be limited to activities or employers, and is valid in the granting state only. [2] Current limits and what it allows in an ophthalmology department: OPEN.PARTIAL

2. United States

QuestionWhat the sources establishStatus and source type
Foreign degreeECFMG certification needs a school listed with an ECFMG sponsor note covering your graduation year, USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK, a clinical-skills pathway, and the final diploma. ECFMG accreditation pages require a WFME-recognised accreditation of the school for recent applicants (the pages are undated; check current policy). Certification is not a licence. [6][7]VERIFIED ECFMG pages (private credentialing body that the system relies on, not a licensing board).
Licence for independent practiceState boards decide. The AMA (a professional association, September 2023) says every jurisdiction requires at least one year of accredited US or Canadian graduate medical education for a foreign graduate, with 12 states at two years and 25 at three. The FSMB state table lists mostly two or three years for IMGs. Both tell you to verify with the board. [8][9]PARTIAL secondary summaries, not law. Your state's current rule: OPEN.
Alternative routes without US residencyA draft FSMB guidance says that, from 2023, eight states enacted additional pathways. Typical conditions: an employment offer, ECFMG certification, foreign postgraduate training "substantially similar" to ACGME programs, an active foreign licence, limited time out of practice, then a provisional licence (two or three years, with supervision recommended) before full licensure. It lists limits: immigration rules, board certification affecting employment and privileges, malpractice cover, and no accepted way to judge "substantially similar". Which states, and the current text of each law: OPEN. [10]PARTIAL draft professional guidance. It is not law, and it does not describe a shortcut for ophthalmology.

These parts came from a machine summary, not quoted text. 3. Brazil (as the destination for a foreign-trained physician, and as the home system)

QuestionWhat the sources establishStatus and source type
Foreign degreeLaw 13,959/2019 creates the national exam for revalidating foreign medical diplomas (Revalida): a theoretical stage and a clinical-skills stage, coordinated by the federal government. Passing the theory stage is needed for the clinical stage. Applicant fees are charged; the clinical fee is capped at the monthly residency stipend. The page shows a 2026 emergency-measure amendment. The question for an experienced LASIK surgeon: in Germany, specialty recognition is assessed individually and partial credit is possible. In the US, the sources read give no way to know how much foreign specialty training would be credited. That part is OPEN and needs the ophthalmology board and the target state board in writing.
