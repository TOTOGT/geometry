Quantum maths

The mathematics under Four Quantum Oracles: five chapters, each derived by hand and checked by a script with controls that must fail.

Sources: Nielsen & Chuang (10th Anniversary Edition), Shor, Cleve–Ekert–Macchiavello–Mosca, Kitaev, Nayak et al.

Chapters
ChapterWhat it derives and checks
QM1 · States, gates, tensor productsUnit vectors, unitary gates, the Pauli algebra, entanglement, no-cloning
QM2 · Measurement and CHSHBorn rule, Schmidt decomposition, CHSH reaching 2√2 and no further
QM3 · Fourier transform and phase estimationThe QFT as matrix and circuit; phase estimation; continued fractions
QM4 · Order finding and ShorThe reduction, the exact distribution, Shor's bounds, the lemma checked to 400
QM5 · Braiding: Fibonacci and IsingBraid relation, finite Ising closure, non-closing Fibonacci, a typo in the source

Every chapter has a qmN-verify.py beside it, standard library only; where the chapter leans on a source PDF the script extracts it from ~/Downloads and prints SKIP, never PASS, if it is not there. The Lean file Orthogenesis/Quantum/QubitAlgebra.lean states the Pauli algebra, a Clifford conjugation and the abstract no-cloning argument; it has not been compiled and is not yet imported from Orthogenesis.lean.

Scope. This is the mathematics, checked at toy sizes on a classical machine. It makes no claim about quantum hardware, error correction at scale, or any application; and it does not treat Shor's discrete-logarithm algorithm, Grover's proof of optimality, the Solovay–Kitaev theorem, or the stabiliser formalism.
