## 1 · The two editorial directives, which override habit

**The story is complex multiplication.** Not verification, not process, not
mistakes. A transcendental function evaluated at special points turns
algebraic; the algebra is a unit group; the unit's norm makes the answer a
whole number. That is the book. Everything else is apparatus.

**The register is Strogatz and Sanderson.** `book4/ch-modular-equations-and-pi.html`
is the worked example of what that means here: open with a puzzle, hold two
numbers in front of the reader, walk away, come back, let the coincidence be
felt before it is explained. Formula first, theory last, one idea per sentence,
second person.

Note for whoever writes next: `book7/ch-strogatz.html` is a ledger ABOUT
Strogatz in a voice nothing like his. It should be the most readable page in
the series and is not. Same for `book7/ch-ramanujan.html` §VI, which has the
whole 9801 story told as proof-blocks instead of as a chase.

**The test for whether an error earns a page:** does it belong to the
mathematics or to the author? It earns a place only if it recurs
independently, to other people, across time. `alpha = k^2` passes — Ramanujan's
k_210 and any modern theta routine make the same error, a century apart. A
non-recursive `os.walk` fails. Most of the instrument-failure material written
earlier today has been cut from Vol IV on this test; Chapter R and Vol XIII
still carry it and should not.

## 2 · The Lean file — state and next step

`book6/lean/VolXI_K0_Floor.lean`, against vendored Mathlib `81a5d257c8`,
toolchain `leanprover/lean4:v4.32.0`. Run from the repo root:

    lake env lean book6/lean/VolXI_K0_Floor.lean

History: 7 errors -> 1 error -> (expected) 0 errors with one declared sorry.

  * Round 1: seven errors, all `unknownIdentifier`. Cause: `GrothendieckGroup`
    is `Algebra.GrothendieckGroup`. Fixed with `open Algebra`.
  * Round 2: one error. Cause: the `to_additive` name is `GrothendieckAddGroup`,
    NOT `AddGrothendieckGroup` — to_additive rewrites the token "Group" into
    "AddGroup" in place rather than prefixing. Settled by grepping the built
    olean (1 hit vs 0), because to_additive generates the name at elaboration
    and it is nowhere in the Mathlib source.
  * `set_option autoImplicit false` is in the file and earned its place: the
    round-2 error read "Unknown identifier" instead of round 1's
    "Function expected ... type ?m.1".

**The one remaining sorry:** `grothendieckAddGroup_nat_equiv_int`, stating
`Nonempty (GrothendieckAddGroup ℕ ≃+ ℤ)`. It does not exist in Mathlib; it was
invented by this project and must be proved or withdrawn. Until it is
discharged `#print axioms` reports `sorryAx` and Vol XI does not clear WP-82's
bar.

Proof route, from the API as read on disk:

    Algebra.GrothendieckGroup.lift : (M →* G) ≃ (GrothendieckGroup M →* G)

whose additive form gives `(ℕ →+ ℤ) ≃ (GrothendieckAddGroup ℕ →+ ℤ)`. Feed it
`Nat.castAddMonoidHom ℤ` to get a hom, then show bijectivity: injectivity from
ℕ being cancellative (`of_injective` is the multiplicative analogue), surjectivity
from every integer being a difference of two naturals. Each piece exists; the
assembly has not been attempted.

**Do not write it blind.** Writing unverifiable Lean is what produced the seven
errors. No Lean toolchain is reachable from the Cowork Linux VM — `elan` is
installed at `~/.elan/bin` but the v4.32.0 toolchain download stalls through the
proxy (abandoned after ~40 minutes). The author's Mac has a working toolchain.
Write a candidate, have it run, iterate on real errors.
