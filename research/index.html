1. Atomic Structure and Computational Physics

Status analytical and numerical; dated retraction (2026-11-26) preserved

Abstract

Retrodiction tests of Opus claims about atomic shell structure, using Thomas–Fermi (TF) thresholds, LDA and LSDA radial codes, and an independent PySCF benchmark. The main outcomes are a correction of earlier TF thresholds and a negative result for the \(\eta_k\) hierarchy as a generator of the periodic table.

Findings

  • Corrected Thomas–Fermi bound-state thresholds for p, d, f, g subshells: \(Z = 7, 26, 67, 135\) (Sturm count, grid-independent, Langer–WKB cross-check) [N]. The first values (2, 18, 64, 164) were a roundoff artefact and are retracted. A bound-state threshold is not an occupied ground-state configuration.
  • The \(\eta_k\) hierarchy, as defined, generates no shell capacities, filling order or closings: no integer sequence built from \(\eta_k\) for \(k = 2..9\) contains the period lengths 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32 [N, negative].
  • Chromium: a five-setting convergence audit gives an unpolarized gap \(E(4s^1 3d^5) - E(4s^2 3d^4)\) of about 92 meV (91.6 to 92.6 meV, all runs converged). Spin polarization reverses the ordering to about \(-2.60\) eV. This is conventional spin-dependent exchange, not a TOGT prediction [N].
  • An independent PySCF exchange-only benchmark agrees with the radial codes for He and Ne within \(10^{-4}\) and \(5\times10^{-4}\) Ha at the largest basis [N].
  • No selection law distinct from energy minimization emerged from the orthogenetic-accessibility analysis [A, negative-leaning].

Limitations

  • The 2.6 eV magnitude is not compared with experiment, and no quantitative accuracy is claimed. High-spin over-stabilization in LSDA is a recalled, unverified tendency [L].
  • Spherical averaging ignores multiplet structure. The Z-continuation selection test is specified but has not been run.
  • The Q.0.5b explanation of period lengths through \(n^2\) capacities is wrong as stated (periods repeat; \(2n^2\) does not).

2. Contact Geometry and Nodal Analysis

Status analytical with numerical checks; no Lean; no implication for the Riemann hypothesis

Abstract

A regularized contact form on the lift of a holomorphic function, and a local classification of the nodal structure of symmetric eigenfunctions at the elliptic point \(z = i\).

Findings

  • For holomorphic \(f\), the form \(\eta = dz + \operatorname{Re} f\, d\operatorname{Im} f\) is smooth through every zero, and \(\eta\wedge d\eta = |f'|^2\, dz\wedge d\sigma\wedge dt\). It is therefore contact exactly where \(f' \neq 0\): at simple zeros, not at multiple zeros [A, N: 26 of 26 script checks].
  • Nodal theorem at \(z = i\) (hypotheses H0 to H4 in the report): the lowest-order Taylor part is \(\alpha\,\operatorname{Re}(w^k)\) with \(k\) even, \(w = x + i(y-1)\) [A]. For real-valued \(f\) with \(\alpha \neq 0\), the zero set near \(i\) is exactly \(k\) analytic curves with tangents at angles \(\pi/(2k) + j\pi/k\) [A].
  • Exceptional quartic case: if \(\mu \neq 10\) and \(a = 0\), the degree-4 part is \(c_{40}\operatorname{Re}(w^4)\) and the degree-5 part is \(-2c_{40}\operatorname{Im}(w^5)\) [A, symbolic].

Limitations

  • Speiser's criterion restated through the zeta lift is a restatement of RH and gives no leverage on it. The nodal theorem is local: local symmetry does not constrain where zeros lie globally.
  • Not established: which \((k, \alpha)\) occur at actual zeros; degrees 6 and above; the case \(\mu = 10\). At the first zeta zero the measured \(a \approx 2.4\times10^{-7} \neq 0\), so \(k = 2\) there.
  • The arithmetic contact form of the π-RH chapter is a different construction from \(\eta\); these findings neither support nor refute that chapter's claims.

3. AXLE Formalization Specifications

Status proposed and untested; specification only; no Lean text written or compiled

Abstract

Five minimal Lean 4 targets extracted from the contact and nodal research, written as specifications so that later formalization is non-vacuous by construction.

Findings

  • T1 Jacobian determinant equals \(\|f'\|^2\). T2 contact nondegeneracy at simple zeros (coefficient level). T3 classification of homogeneous harmonic polynomials of degree 2 and 4. T4 the quartic reduction lemma. T5 energy dissipation under a positive-semidefinite mobility.
  • Speiser's criterion stays an explicit external hypothesis, never an axiom.

Limitations

  • The duplicate check against existing AXLE and geometry files used project records only. Mathlib names in the specification are unchecked.
  • No Lean statement has been compiled, so nothing in this section may be cited as verified.

4. Computational Jurisprudence

Status Phase I design and measurement report; al-Khwārizmī/inheritance link unverified

Abstract

A new workstream on the mathematics of legal language and norms: a corpus audit of a historical legal lexicon, a pilot of 100 concepts with mechanical features only, a proposed ontology (sense records, a typed knowledge graph, Hohfeldian norm records), and a bridge from the topographical-orthogenetic reading to legal availability versus practical accessibility.

Findings

  • The source is a third-party OCR scan of a 1968 edition and is treated as copyrighted; the corpus, extracted definitions and pilot data are kept private [N].
  • Extraction audit: two-column layout needs column-aware parsing; headword and citation counts are sized as lower bounds or estimates [N].
  • Proposed: the gap between the admissible set \(A_{j,t}\) and the practically accessible set \(B \subseteq A_{j,t}\) as the measurable object. This is a definition, not a result [A].
  • Seven specification-only formal statements (L1 to L7) model rule systems; they would verify models, never legal truth [S].

Limitations

  • No legal advice. Historical dictionary senses are inputs to terminology only and never override statutes, regulations, controlling decisions or official form instructions.
  • The connection to al-Khwārizmī and the algebra of inheritance has not been checked against primary or scholarly sources. Human annotation of rights, duties and exceptions has not been done.
  • Section 4 of the Phase I report predates the volume reservation below; the reservation is recorded on the Book XXXIV page.
