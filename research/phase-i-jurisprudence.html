2. Pilot of 100 concepts [N, mechanical features only]

  • Selection (seed 20261126): 75 stratified random entries from the systematic 120-page sample (the Latin/French strata under-filled because language tags are inconsistently present; fill-ins are English headwords) + 25 thematic entries chosen before looking at their text: LEGACY, DEVISE, BEQUEST, INTESTACY, HEIR, PER STIRPES, PER CAPITA, DESCENT, DISTRIBUTION, EXECUTOR, TESTAMENT, LEGITIME, COLLATION, FALCIDIAN PORTION, ESCHEAT, FIDUCIARY, OBLIGATION, DUTY, RIGHT, PRIVILEGE, LIABILITY, CONTRACT, CONDITION, PROHIBITION, LICENSE (succession and Hohfeld-type vocabulary). Parser missed ADMINISTRATOR, WILL, HEREDITAS, TRUST, COVENANT (format variants).
  • Output (private, not published): a CSV with headword, language tag, PDF page, column, length, case-citation count, "See" count, field tags (Civil law/Eng. law/Practice/…), deontic-cue words (shall, must, duty, entitled, prohibited, may not, …), OCR-suspect flag. No definition text is stored.
  • Descriptive results (n=100; pilot only): median 298 characters; 49% have ≥1 case citation; 27% contain a deontic cue word; 30% carry a field tag; 10% flagged OCR-suspect. Within the 25 thematic entries, long entries (LEGACY 3,244 chars, CONTRACT 2,908, PROHIBITION 2,633, CONDITION 2,504) are heavy in case citations, short ones (DEVISE 146, EXECUTOR 93 — likely truncated) are not. These numbers say nothing about legal meaning; they size the extraction problem.
  • Not yet done: human annotation of rights/duties/permissions, preconditions, exceptions (these require legal judgment; fields are left blank by design).

3. Proposed mathematical ontology (definitions first; established vs new marked)

  1. Sense records (established practice, lexicography): s=(headword w, sense id, language, field tag, source=(edition, page, column), cited authorities). Finite set 𝒮.
  2. Meaning relation. The proposed 𝒮(w,c,j,t)⊆ℳ is defined as a *finite relation* R ⊆ W×C×J×T×ℳ where ℳ=senses of w in 𝒮 (a database, not a manifold); C is a set of context features with a partial order (more specific ⪰ more general), J a jurisdiction tree, T a time line with intervals. Any "geometry of meaning" statement waits until a metric is defined (graph distance on the sense graph, or cosine similarity under a named embedding model); until then it is a relation/poset, not geometry [A].
  3. Knowledge graph G=(V,E,λ,π): V = senses ∪ authorities (cases, statutes, treatises) ∪ norms; E = typed directed edges (cross-reference "See", translation-of Latin→English, genus-species, cites, derives-from) with provenance π and validity (jurisdiction, [t₀,t₁)) [established: multigraph].
  4. Norm records. n=(modality, action, actor, condition φ, exceptions, jurisdiction, validity interval, authority). Deontic logic: in standard deontic logic F(a)≡O(¬a) and P(a)≡¬O(¬a), so O,P,F are interdefinable; they become genuinely separate only in richer systems (strong vs weak permission, Hohfeld). Recommendation: use Hohfeldian correlatives (right–duty, privilege–no-right, power–liability, immunity–disability) as the base vocabulary for rights, since the pilot vocabulary (RIGHT, PRIVILEGE, LIABILITY, DUTY) is exactly this [L: Hohfeld 1913, recalled]. Add defeasibility (priority: lex superior, lex specialis, lex posterior) [established: defeasible logic/argumentation frameworks].
  5. TOGT bridge (definition, not a result). Admissible set A_{j,t}={x∈M : C_i(x;j,t) ∀i}; legal availability = membership in A_{j,t}; practical accessibility B ⊆ A: states reachable within cost/time/information budgets (a reachability problem on the transition system with weights). The gap A\B is the measurable object. Testable form: for pretrial release, with money bail b and defendant liquidity ℓ, release is *legally available* for all b but *accessible* only if ℓ+credit ≥ premium(b); the fraction of detainees with legally available but unaffordable release is an empirical quantity (data dictionary file PretrialReleaseDataDictionary.xlsx — not analysed; Book 26 ch15–16 already treat shadow prices and bail-not-posted). Hypotheses must name the dataset and prediction before analysis. This is a modeling definition plus an empirical program, not a derivation from the dictionary.

    Established mathematics vs new: labelled transition systems, Allen interval algebra, SDL, Hohfeld, Dung argumentation, defeasible logic are established; the only new element is the TOGT reading (A vs B, selection among admissible paths) and it has no result yet.

5. AI for Legal (G6) architecture [A]

Layers (as specified in the mandate), mapped to what exists (project status doc, 2026-11-25: AR-11 fully mapped, I-751 Part 1 and I-829 Part 2 subsets, 38–41 passing tests, provenance, multilingual dates, ambiguous dates never guessed, SSN/signature never mapped, tested not proved, rules marked UNVERIFIED need counsel): 1 NLU (extractor; currently rule-based) → 2 structured facts with provenance → 3 terminology/ontology (this workstream) → 4 retrieval of *current* authoritative sources → 5 deterministic rule checking (existing validators) → 6 uncertainty detection → 7 human review → 8 document generation. Hard rules: historical dictionary senses are inputs to layer 3 only; they never override statutes, regulations, controlling decisions or official form instructions; no remedy selection, no binding determinations, no submission.

6. Initial AXLE statements (specification only; nothing written or compiled; verify only models, never legal truth)

  • L1 Finite rule engine: state space S finite, rules R : Finset (S → Option S); theorem: if every rule preserves invariant I then I holds on all states reachable from an I-state (induction). Non-vacuous: require a rule that actually fires.
  • L2 Temporal validity: rule valid on interval [t₀,t₁); theorem: applicability is monotone in interval inclusion; no rule applies outside its interval.
  • L3 Contradictory obligations: decidable checker conflict : NormSet → Bool; theorem conflict ns = false ↔ ¬∃ s a, O a ∈ ns.at s ∧ F a ∈ ns.at s (soundness and completeness of the model-level checker; says nothing about whether a real statute conflicts).
  • L4 Priority resolution: with a strict total order on conflicting rules, the winning set is unique (determinism).
  • L5 Provenance completeness: each derived conclusion carries a derivation tree whose leaves are source records; theorem: the engine never returns a conclusion without a nonempty provenance.
  • L6 Deterministic form validation: validator is total and idempotent; changed fields = mapped fields (the tested property of the prototype, now as a theorem about the model).
  • L7 (illustrative, links to the algebra-and-inheritance premise) Share conservation: for estate E and shares s_i ≥0 with Σs_i=1, allocations E·s_i sum to E; for fixed fractions summing to >1 a proportional reduction restores sum E (the classical "'awl" idea in inheritance law is, as I recall, of this kind; jurisdiction-specific, not legal advice). Algebraic and trivially checkable; its value is as a clean first end-to-end Lean example.

7. History note: algebra and inheritance [L, not verified]

The stated premise (algebra arose from a legal/partition need) matches what I recall of al-Khwārizmī's *Kitāb al-jabr wa'l-muqābala* (c. 820): his stated aim includes inheritance, legacies, partition, lawsuits and trade, and the book has sections on legacies/inheritance problems. A search returned MacTutor, Encyclopedia.com and Library of Congress pages by title only; I did not read them. Verify against a primary translation (e.g. Rosen 1831) before using it in the volume's prologue; the claim "born out of" is an interpretive summary, to be phrased "in the motivation and worked examples of".

8. Roadmap

  1. Rights check and decision on corpus base (1968 scan private; 1891/1910 as redistributable comparison base) — needs counsel/Copyright Office check.
  2. Column-aware, font-aware parser (italics for sub-entries); full headword census; citation-pattern validator; 300-page human audit sample.
  3. Annotate the 100-concept pilot (human lawyer/linguist review) for rights/duties/exceptions; measure inter-annotator agreement.
  4. Build the sense graph for the succession cluster; implement L1–L7 in a standalone Lean folder (not in the AXLE site repo).
  5. A–B gap study design for pretrial release with named dataset and preregistered prediction.
  6. Multilingual (EN/PT/ES) glosses with qualified human review.

    Negative/uncertain findings so far: column interleaving, entry truncation, OCR noise, unclear rights; no semantic result exists yet.
