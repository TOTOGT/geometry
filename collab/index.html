How a collaboration works here

  1. Every statement is tagged as proved, cited, computed, conjecture or open, so a reader can see at once what rests on what.
  2. Every number comes from a script that sits beside the page and downloads in one click. The script prints PASS or FAIL for each claim and says, in its last block, what it does not establish.
  3. Collaborators are named only with their consent, and their views are never stated for them. A correction or idea they contribute is credited to them by name.
  4. Versions are dated and never changed silently. A correction goes in a dated box on the page, and the older PDF stays in the history.
  5. Languages follow the readers. Notes are written in English and, where useful, in Portuguese (ABNT references) and French.
  6. Licensing. Text is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and code is MIT, as in the rest of the repository. Joint work is licensed as agreed with the collaborator.
