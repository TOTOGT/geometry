Kept for the record

The Second Edition is not superseded in every respect. Version 4 revised the mathematics, but it dropped the note “A note on ‘orthogenesis’” — the passage separating orthogonal genesis from the dead nineteenth-century biological theory of the same name, and reading Waddington’s canalisation as a curvature picture. That note currently exists only in the Second Edition below. Until it is carried forward, this page keeps the older edition reachable rather than quietly retiring it.
