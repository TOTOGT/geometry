How to read the tags

Every result in the paper carries one of three tags. Version 4 made the first one strict.

TagMeaning in Version 4
TKernel-checked. The Lean declaration named with the result is in a deposited file, at a stated commit and Lean/Mathlib pin, and #print axioms reports only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. A (T) certifies the Lean statement, not the prose around it.
SA proof or proof sketch in the text, not kernel-checked — including results whose nearest Lean declaration does not state the result itself.
CConjecture.

Before Version 4, (T) meant “proved without sorry in the AXLE engine”, and Part III widened it further to “or algebraically complete and ready for formalisation”. Under that reading a tag could point at a file that was never compiled, or at no declaration at all.

The four parts

Part I · Mathematical foundations

The operator sequence \(C \to K \to F \to U\) (compression, curvature, fold, unfolding) on a Riemannian manifold, under six stated assumptions; the Whitney \(A_1\)–\(A_3\) singularity classification; symplectic preservation for the fold map; a variational characterization of the unfolding; and the g-series lock-in threshold \(g = 33\). This is the material of Volume I.

Part III · Seven proofs that \(\eta \approx 1.8393\) is the characteristic root

The dm³ fold recurrence \(w(k+3)=w(k+2)+w(k+1)+w(k)\) grows at the Tribonacci constant \(\eta\), the real root of \(x^3-x^2-x-1=0\). The paper gives seven arguments; each has a companion page.

  1. Operator algebra · T?
    Tagged (T); no Lean declaration named.
  2. Distribution theory · T?
    Tagged (T); no Lean declaration named.
  3. Whitney \(A_1\) catastrophe cascade · S
    Recurrence coefficients rest on an admitted step, whitneyFold_conditional.
  4. Contact geometry · T in part
    Existence, \(1<\eta<2\) and uniqueness in \((1,2)\) are kernel-checked for the constructed root: η_characteristic, η_gt_one, η_lt_two, η_unique_in_Ioo (TribonacciMeasure.lean). Uniqueness is algebraic: \((x-\eta)\,[x(x-1)+\eta(\eta-1)+(x\eta-1)] = 0\). The cohomology count it also cites (reeb_orbit_is_integral) does not exist as a declaration.
  5. Perron–Frobenius spectral theory · T?
    Tagged (T); no Lean declaration named.
  6. Certified numerical computation · T?
    Tagged (T) against def eta := 1.839286755214161 in AXLE_v8.1.lean — a decimal literal, not a proof about the root. V4 §23 withdraws that citation for \(\eta\).
  7. Fisher information geometry · T in part
    Cites reeb_orbit_is_integral (not a declaration) and whitneyFold_conditional (an admit).

“Seven independent proofs” is the paper’s phrase. Whether the seven are independent — rather than seven derivations sharing a hypothesis, such as the three-step traversal that selects \(n=3\) — is not examined in Version 4.

Part IV · Applications to nuclear matter

Confinement, hadronization and the onset of coherent structure in the quark–gluon plasma are modelled as iterated fold events under a conjectural effective contact form \(\alpha_{\mathrm{QCD}}\) C. Five falsifiable conditions are stated. The triple-alpha process is examined in the same conjectural register: the paper proves that no map reproducing its temperature sensitivity can be both parameter-free and derived from the contact form, and derives an Arrhenius factor only in a scoped local regime near the neutral point. Where the correspondence is analogy rather than derivation, the paper says so (for example, the link between \(g = 33\) and a nuclear multiplicity is labelled analogical).

Known gaps, stated rather than hidden

(T) tags without a deposited declaration Under the Version 4 legend, the tags marked T? above are unsupported: Proofs 1, 2, 5 and 6, the contact normal form, and the reeb_orbit_is_integral citations in Proofs 4 and 7 and the summary table (no declaration of that name exists in CatGT_Main.lean). main_v7.lean, DustyPlasma.lean and AXLE_v8.1.lean are not deposited. Retagging these as (S) is the pending correction.
A vacuous structure field UnfoldOp.stable_branch in PrincipiaVol1.lean holds at \(n = 0\) for every map. The file proves this itself (unfold_stable_branch_is_vacuous), and no result in the paper depends on it. A repaired form requiring \(n > 0\) is in TOTOGT/io Theorem53NonCommutativity.lean (commit 8cdd05f); it states that image points are periodic, not that anything is stable or attracting.
What a green axiom report does not say A clean #print axioms rules out sorry. It does not show that the Lean statement is the claim the prose makes. helical_selectivity, for instance, is the implication \(r^2 \le a^2J/\lambda \Rightarrow r \le r^*\) — square-root monotonicity — whatever the surrounding text says about helical branches.
