Pablo

Pablo Nogueira Grossi is the founder and sole member of G6 LLC in Newark, New Jersey, and the author of the Principia Orthogona series, published on Zenodo. He holds a B.A. in Economics (2013) and a B.B.A. in Finance (2015) from Baruch College, CUNY, and worked for many years in banking in New York (Banco do Brasil, JPMorgan Chase) before moving into independent research.
