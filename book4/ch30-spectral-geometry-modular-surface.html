Spectral Geometry of the Modular Surface

Eigenvalues, continuous spectrum, scattering resonances and the zeros of ζ: what is classical, what is hand-derived here, and what is open

Status. Second-edition CANDIDATE chapter, no laboratories. Classical results are cited from memory (no primary source was read in this session); hand derivations and 40-digit numerics are this chapter's own contribution; the full trace formula, any regularized spectral zeta, and anything bearing on RH are OPEN. Not human-reviewed; not formalized. It does not replace GS-2 and does not prove or reduce RH. Published 2026-11-28 as a candidate under author review. 1 · The surface and its area

The modular surface M=PSL₂(ℤ)\H has the fundamental domain {|x|≤½, |z|≥1}. Its hyperbolic area is π/3, it has two cone points (orders 2 at i and 3 at ρ) and one cusp (GS3-AREA). The Laplacian is Δ=−y²(∂ₓ²+∂ᵧ²).

2 · Spectral decomposition

L²(M) splits into the constants, the Maass cusp forms, and the continuous part spanned by Eisenstein series (GS3-SPECTRUM). The cusp forms have discrete eigenvalues ¼+r_j² with Weyl growth T²/12 (GS3-WEYL); the first parameter is recalled as r₁≈9.5337, not recomputed.

3 · Maass forms and symmetry at the elliptic points

Odd Maass forms vanish at i and ρ by symmetry, and the vanishing order of any eigenfunction at a point with stabilizer of order m is a multiple of m (GS3-SYMVAN). These are the facts behind the local nodal pictures of the PENC chapter.

4 · Eisenstein series and the continuous spectrum

With Λ the completed Riemann zeta, E=Λ(2s)E satisfies E(z,s)=E*(z,1−s) and has an explicit Fourier expansion (GS3-EIS). It is the generalized eigenfunction for the continuous spectrum on s=½+ir.

5 · The scattering coefficient and ζ

The ratio φ(s)=Λ(2s−1)/Λ(2s) satisfies φ(s)φ(1−s)=1, |φ|=1 on the line and has a pole at s=1 of residue 3/π=1/Area (GS3-PHI). Numerical checks at four points agree (GS3-PHINUM):

The zeros of ζ enter as poles of φ at s=ρ/2, i.e. as resonances, and four notions must be kept apart (GS3-FOURFOLD):

notion where it lives real λ?
discrete eigenvalue λ=¼+r², r real yes
continuous spectrum s=½+ir, λ∈[¼,∞) yes
scattering resonance pole of φ at s=ρ/2 no (Im λ≠0)
arithmetic zero ρ, zero of ζ not an eigenvalue of anything here

A numerical look at growth near the first resonance is in GS3-RESNUM.

6 · Elliptic-point symmetry and nodal geometry

At i and ρ the Eisenstein series factor into ζ times a Dirichlet L-function (GS3-ELLIP, proved as PN-FACTOR in PENC-1). A 40-digit re-run reproduces the Arb-certified constants at the first zero (GS3-NODALNUM).

8 · Regularized heat trace and a spectral zeta

The heat operator on M is not trace class (GS3-NONTRACE). The exact identity term is I(t)=(e^{−t/4}/12)∫_ℝ e^{−tr²} r tanh(πr) dr; the integral is 1/t−1/12+O(t) as t↓0, so I(t)=(1/12)(1/t−1/12+O(t))e^{−t/4}. 9 · Selberg trace formula and closed geodesics

The shortest primitive closed geodesic has length 2log((3+√5)/2)≈1.9248473 (GS3-GEOD). The full trace formula with all constants is not derived (GS3-TRACE-FULL).

10 · Arithmetic connections and open questions

Everything here is either classical, a hand derivation of an identity, or a numerical check. The relation to RH is not claimed (GS3-RHLINK). Open: choice of regularized zeta, full trace formula, prior art, human review.
