Discrete-to-Continuum Spectral Limits: Subtracting the Microscopic Part

Exact lattice zeta sums at nonpositive integers, a bulk subtraction fixed by the lattice itself, and what is still open

Status. Hand proofs for the prescribed periodic square lattice at s=0 and negative integers, exact-checked for m≤8; numerics at real s; complex-domain convergence, zeros, triangular lattice and emergent lattices are OPEN. Not human-reviewed; not formalized; prior art not read. Not a theory of gravity.

1 · The problem with taking limits

Take the periodic square lattice with N² vertices as an approximation of the unit torus. Its rescaled Laplacian has eigenvalues λ_N(k,j)=4N²[sin²(πk/N)+sin²(πj/N)], and for fixed (k,j) these converge to the continuum values 4π²(k²+j²). Form the spectral zeta functions Z_N(s)=Σλ_N^{−s} and Z(s)=Σ(4π²(k²+j²))^{−s}=4(4π²)^{−s}ζ(s)L(s,χ_{−4}), nonzero modes only. Convergence Z_N→Z holds for Re s>1 (proved in the earlier chapter GS-1), but outside that half-plane the lattice sums are dominated by microscopic structure:

quantity lattice continuum (continued)
Z(0) N²−1 −1
Z(−1) 4N⁴ 0
Z(−2) 20N⁶ (N≥3) 0

The divergent lattice terms are not random: they are N^{2−2s} times a fixed number determined by the lattice symbol.

2 · Subtracting the bulk, with nothing to fit

Let a(x,y)=4sin²(πx)+4sin²(πy) be the dimensionless symbol and C(s)=∬a^{−s} its bulk integral, convergent for Re s<1 (GS2-BULK). Define

Z_N^ren(s) = Z_N(s) − N^{2−2s}C(s).

The coefficient C(s) comes from the lattice, not from the desired answer. Then Z_N^ren(0)=−1 and Z_N^ren(−m)=0, equal to the continuum values (GS2-RENZERO). 3 · The exact theorem at negative integers

Write P(z,w)=4−z−z⁻¹−w−w⁻¹. At the Fourier modes z^N=w^N=1 the Laplacian eigenvalues are N²P(z,w), so Z_N(−m)=N^{2m}Σ_{z^N=w^N=1}P(z,w)^m (the zero mode contributes nothing for m≥1). Since Σ_{z^N=1}z^j=N when N∣j and 0 otherwise, every nonconstant Laurent monomial of P^m drops out as soon as N>m (its exponents are at most m in absolute value), leaving

Z_N(−m) = N^{2m+2}·[z⁰w⁰]P^m = N^{2m+2}·C(−m) (N>m).

The constant coefficient is C(−m)=Σ_{j}binom(m,2j)4^{m−2j}binom(2j,j)²: 4, 20, 112, 676, 4304, 28496, … (GS2-NEGINT). It equals the bulk integral at s=−m, so the subtraction cancels the lattice sum exactly. The continuum side vanishes because ζ and L(s,χ_{−4}) have trivial zeros at all negative integers between them (GS2-RENZERO).

A wrong formula, recorded. A first version of this theorem used binom(2m,m)² for the constant. That counts closed walks of the adjacency operator and agrees with the Laplacian constant only at m=1. It is withdrawn (GS2-ERRATUM); the table below is from an exact integer computation.

4 · What the numerics add — and what they do not

At real s<1 the renormalized sum converges to Z(s) as N grows, with error shrinking like N^{−2} (real s only, double precision; GS2-REALNUM). That is consistent with the open conjecture of locally uniform convergence on Re s<1 (GS2-COMPLEX), which would make Hurwitz's and Rouché's theorems available for the zeros of the limit — a function containing ζ and L(s,χ_{−4}) — but would not prove the Riemann Hypothesis. Zero convergence in the right half-plane Re s>1 is trivial (there are no zeros there); the interesting statement lives in Re s<1 and is not established.

5 · A heat-trace route to the complex plane

The bulk term has a closed form on the lattice: B_N(t)=N²e^{−4N²t}I₀(2N²t)², the return probability of the walk on the infinite lattice summed over N² sites; for fixed t it tends to the local Euclidean value 1/(4πt) (GS2-HEAT). What is left, W_N=H_N−B_N, is the contribution of paths that wind around the torus. The Mellin transform of K_N=H_N−B_N−1 is exactly Z_N^ren(s) for 0<Re s<1 (checked numerically at one point), and splitting the integral at t=1 isolates the zero mode as −1/s. A winding path needs at least N jumps, which suppresses W_N for t≪1/N (GS2-UV). What is missing is a uniform bound for the intermediate scales and the large-time bound; with them, dominated convergence would give locally uniform convergence of Z_N^ren on Re s<1 (GS2-COMPLEX). Closely related work exists, including an expansion of the discrete zeta function in the critical strip (GS2-PRIOR), so this may be known; only abstracts were read.

6 · Scope, and what is not claimed

  • Prescribed lattices only. Nothing is said about lattices that emerge from a dynamics (GS2-EMERGENT).
  • Beyond nearest-neighbour squares: the cancellation holds for any finite-range periodic Laplacian, and the triangular lattice is worked out with exact coefficients (GS2-GENERAL, GS2-TRI); the table of coefficients is in the manuscript and in renorm_general_check.json. One entry in a transferred table was wrong and is withdrawn (GS2-ERRATUM2).
  • Prior art: not read (GS2-PRIOR).
  • Physics: the "microscopic versus global" reading is an analogy, not a theory of quantum gravity (GS2-QG).
  • Formalization: none. The statements are hand proofs plus exact integer checks.
