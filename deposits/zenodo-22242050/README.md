## What this is

A layered model of lexical generativity in English: how a finite lexical inventory
supports an unbounded range of context-sensitive meanings in use. The model distinguishes
three layers — stored semantic primitives and qualia structure; argument-structure
templates licensed by class membership; and event-type composition rules governing
productive extension — and argues that apparent lexicographic irregularities are
systematic consequences of a small set of statable generative principles.

The empirical core is twelve English verb classes, the systematic polysemy of English
nouns, and the contested semantics of English spatial prepositions, read against three
standard resources: Levin (1993), FrameNet (Fillmore, Johnson & Petruck 2003), and
VerbNet (Kipper et al. 2008).

The layering corresponds to the operator chain C → K → F → U of the *Principia
Orthogona* series: compression identifies the head sense, curvature gates which
alternations are licensed, the fold sets sense count, and unfolding stabilises one
branch under contextual support. That correspondence is the connection to the wider
programme; the linguistic argument stands on its own terms and uses entirely standard
method.

## Peer-review status and attribution

This is a preprint. It has not undergone peer review, and its findings should be read
accordingly.

A related manuscript — *Towards a Predictive Architecture for Dictionary Entry Design:
The Operator Chain C → K → F → U as a Generative Model for Lexicographic Sense Division
and Alternation Prediction* — has been prepared for the *International Journal of
Lexicography* (Oxford University Press) in anonymised form for that journal's
double-blind process. It is a different, shorter paper with a narrower claim: it
addresses dictionary entry design directly, where the present article addresses
generativity in the lexicon generally. The two share the operator-chain framework and
part of the verb-class evidence.

The present deposit is the attributed, general version. It is deposited under its own
DOI so that the empirical work is citable and dated independently of the journal
process.
