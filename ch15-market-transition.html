Theorem 4.3

Multifractal regime shift

At a dm³ fold event, the price process exhibits multifractal structure with generalised Hölder exponent spectrum:

α(q) = d_f + (q − 1) · τ(q)/qd_f ≈ 1.7–1.9 · NYSE TAQ (10⁹ tick records), Binance perpetual futures 2019–2023

Multifractality in price series is not itself a new observation — it goes back to Mandelbrot. What is claimed here is the value of d_f and its derivation from μ_max and the compression ratio, rather than its measurement after the fact.
