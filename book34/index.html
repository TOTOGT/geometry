Scope

The volume collects research on the language, structure and logic of law as mathematical objects, and is the scientific foundation intended for AI for Legal by G6. Its evidence base is kept in the research directory; this page is the exposition and roadmap.

Roadmap

  • Historical linguistics. Etymology and semantic change of legal terms (Latin, French, English). Exists: Phase I extraction audit of language tags. Not done: lexicon counts, change-over-time analysis, any claim about the origin of terms.
  • Legal semantics. Sense records, context features, jurisdiction and time as a finite relation (a database, not a geometry of meaning until a metric is defined). Exists: proposed ontology and a 100-concept mechanical pilot. Not done: human annotation of rights, duties, permissions and exceptions.
  • Algebra and inheritance. Algebraic structure of succession rules (per stirpes, per capita, legitime, collation). Exists: a list of thematic entries chosen for the pilot. The link to al-Khwārizmī and the algebra of inheritance is unverified: it has not been checked against primary or scholarly sources.
  • Computational mathematics. Transition systems, Allen interval algebra, reachability with cost, time and information budgets. Exists: design only. Not done: any implementation beyond the mechanical pilot features.
  • Mathematical logic. Standard deontic logic and Hohfeldian correlatives as the base vocabulary for rights and duties; defeasible and argumentation frameworks as candidates. Exists: a recommendation in the Phase I report. Not done: a chosen logic or any proof.
  • Legal ontologies. A typed knowledge graph of senses, authorities, cases, statutes and norms with provenance. Exists: schema proposal. Not done: any populated graph.
  • Formal verification. Seven specification-only statements (L1 to L7) for finite rule engines and related models. Nothing is written or compiled in Lean; verification would apply to models of rule systems, never to legal truth. Targets are tracked in the AXLE specification page.
  • Topographical Orthogenetics (TOGT). Proposed bridge: the admissible set \(A_{j,t}\) versus the practically accessible set \(B \subseteq A_{j,t}\); the gap is the measurable object. A definition with no result yet.
  • AI for Legal — by G6. A layered architecture from extraction to human review. Historical dictionary senses are inputs to terminology only and never override statutes, regulations, controlling decisions or official instructions. Not legal advice; no remedy selection.

Next proposed steps (none started)

  • Confirm the rights position of the 1891 and 1910 editions (recalled as pre-1931 publications; not verified) before any use of their text.
  • Build a column-aware parser with italic and font information, then a headword census and citation validation, all on the private corpus.
  • Human annotation of the pilot by a person with legal training.
  • Choose a logic and fix a metric before any “geometry of meaning” statement is made.
  • Write and compile L1 to L7 only after the specification review; report results as models, not legal truth.

What is private

The Black’s Law Dictionary scan, its OCR text, extracted definitions, extraction scripts and the 100-concept pilot data are not published and are not in this repository. The Phase I report contains no dictionary text.
