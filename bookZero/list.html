  7. 1994

    Punk rock, bass, and the Grêmio Estudantil

    StatedBy 1994 he had decided to play with the band instead of continuing the music school program. Punk rock felt like his thing.

    StatedThe band was called MOFO.

    StatedThe kids from CASEB made him a bass player.

    StatedHe revived the Grêmio Estudantil (student union) at CASEB, served as its cultural director, and planned a concert for the Festa Junina.

    StatedThe concert happened, his parents saw him play, and he remembers it as funny.

    StatedMemory: another band played too. He also remembers a kid they called Turco, who he doesn’t think was Turkish. He can’t remember the other band’s name.
