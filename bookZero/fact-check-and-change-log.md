## Corrected in the text (6 edits, nothing else touched)
1. **Pureza Lopes Loyola**: the draft said 1996 and that she "found him alive." Sources (IPS News) give 1993; her son Antônio Abel, 19, was held 38 months and came home on his own, sick. Now says so.
2. **Brazil rescues**: 2,772 in 2025 confirmed (Ministry of Labour / Agência Gov). "More than 68,000 since 1995" could not be confirmed; now "more than 65,000 before that" (CUT). "Three hundred and four cases" in public administration is 304 *workers*, second-highest sector after masonry (601). Reworded.
3. **Cocoa**: 1.56 million children confirmed (NORC, 2018–19); about 95% in hazardous work. The 70%-by-2020 target came from the 2010 Declaration of Joint Action, not the 2001 protocol. Reworded.
4. **Mars, Newark**: the draft said it closed in 2023 and moved the global head office to Chicago. Reported July 2026: 307 jobs (state layoff notice), Newark HQ to close by end 2027, consolidation in Chicago where the global HQ already was. Reworded. "Hackettstown plant stays" and "about eighty-five years" not independently checked.
5. **Walters, 11 Sept 1973**: the War on the Rocks piece on the diaries places him in Palm Beach 8–12 Sept, driving to Miami for dinner on the 11th. No "holiday" in the source; only one historian (Jonathan Haslam) is named there, not "several." Reworded.
