What the order says

Executive Order 14303, Restoring Gold Standard Science, was signed on 23 May 2025 and published at 90 FR 22601. Section 3(a) defines Gold Standard Science as science conducted in a manner that is (i) reproducible; (ii) transparent; (iii) communicative of error and uncertainty; (iv) collaborative and interdisciplinary; (v) skeptical of its findings and assumptions; (vi) structured for falsifiability of hypotheses; (vii) subject to unbiased peer review; (viii) accepting of negative results as positive outcomes; and (ix) without conflicts of interest. The order directs federal agencies; it does not bind private authors or publishers.

Why this series follows it

Nothing requires these books to meet the order. The author adopted its nine tenets on 27 September 2026 as a public bar that anyone can check a page against, set by someone other than the author. The series is written with machine assistance and publishes its own corrections; a bar fixed outside the series keeps the question "how good is this page?" from being answered by the series' own enthusiasm. The rule is that a reader can run the page, and the page says how well it meets the bar.

This is a self-assessment, not a certification. No agency has reviewed these pages. Each tenet is scored from something a reader can see on the page or beside it, by a script whose rules are printed below. Tenet (vii) is never self-claimed: it counts only when an independent journal has published the work. Tenet (ix) is shown as a disclosure.
